# L20 - Enterprise Product Overview

Use this layout for enterprise platforms, product suites, infrastructure products, and trust-heavy product overview pages.

## Structure

- Product navigation with platform, solutions, security, customers, docs, and demo paths.
- Hero section focused on platform promise, governance, and enterprise risk reduction.
- Early security and procurement cues such as SOC 2, SLA, SSO, SCIM, and regional controls.
- Product visual that shows layered capabilities, integrations, and live environment context.
- Module cards for control plane, workflow engine, insight layer, and integration fabric.
- Proof row for security, implementation, and scale.
- Buyer-path cards for commercial buyers, technical evaluators, and administrators.

## Required States

- Security, governance, and scale cues must be visible early.
- Separate commercial proof, technical proof, and admin proof so the page can serve multiple enterprise stakeholders.
- Clearly separate platform overview from feature details.
- Provide paths for buyers, technical evaluators, and existing customers.
- Product claims should be supported by concrete modules, controls, integrations, or implementation paths.

## Avoid

- Consumer-style product pages that ignore trust and compliance.
- Too much animation around critical product claims.
- Hero visuals that look impressive but do not explain the product.
- Copying the sample module names when the real product differs.
