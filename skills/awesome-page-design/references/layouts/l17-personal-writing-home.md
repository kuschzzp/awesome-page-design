# L17 - Personal Writing Home

Use this layout for blogs, newsletters, independent researchers, creators, and personal knowledge sites.

## Structure

- Author profile or publication identity in a persistent side area.
- Main article feed with dates, titles, summaries, and topics.
- Topic index or newsletter subscription area.
- Optional featured essay at the top.

## Required States

- Preserve reading rhythm and text hierarchy.
- Include empty archive, loading feed, and no-results states.
- Keep subscription actions present but not disruptive.

## Avoid

- Magazine layouts when the site is primarily one writer.
- Too many cards per row, which weakens reading flow.
- Hiding article dates or topics.
