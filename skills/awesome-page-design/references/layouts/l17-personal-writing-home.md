# L17 - Personal Writing Home

Use this layout for blogs, newsletters, independent researchers, creators, and personal knowledge sites.

## Structure

- Author profile or publication identity in a persistent side area.
- Side navigation for latest essays, archive, and about information.
- Essay-led main column with a strong publication headline.
- Featured essay card followed by smaller recent essay cards and an archive list.
- Newsletter/subscription band integrated into the reading flow.
- Topic index, popular essays, and reading lists in a right-side rail.
- Author profile, reader count, cadence, and subscription action in a side area.

## Required States

- Preserve reading rhythm and text hierarchy.
- Archive rows should keep date, title, and summary in stable columns for fast scanning.
- Include empty archive, loading feed, and no-results states.
- Keep subscription actions present but not disruptive.
- Dates, topic labels, and reading time should remain visible enough for scanning.

## Avoid

- Magazine layouts when the site is primarily one writer.
- Too many cards per row, which weakens reading flow.
- Letting newsletter or topic modules overpower the writing itself.
- Hiding article dates or topics.
