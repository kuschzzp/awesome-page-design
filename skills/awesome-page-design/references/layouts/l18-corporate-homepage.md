# L18 - Corporate Homepage

Use this layout for B2B companies, professional services firms, and corporate presence websites.

## Structure

- Corporate navigation with clear contact or sales action and simple buyer paths.
- Trust-led hero: what the company does, for whom, and why it matters.
- First-viewport product, operations, or service visual that makes the company offer concrete.
- Customer logo or named-proof row close to the hero.
- Proof strip with measurable credibility, shown as separate evidence cards.
- Solution or audience cards that route visitors to the next page.
- Industry or customer story cards with specific outcomes and clear next steps.

## Required States

- First viewport must communicate company category, audience, and offer.
- Include trust proof, not only marketing claims.
- Use numbers, named customer types, industry contexts, or outcome statements as credibility evidence.
- Mobile layout should keep contact/sales action easy to find.
- Avoid leaving the hero half empty; use the secondary column for product, operations, or proof visuals.

## Avoid

- Abstract hero copy that never says what the company does.
- Stock-like feature grids with no proof.
- Treating corporate pages like SaaS app dashboards.
- Letting the hero become a half-empty headline with a decorative image.
