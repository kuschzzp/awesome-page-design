# L18 - Corporate Homepage

Use this layout for B2B companies, professional services firms, and corporate presence websites.

## Structure

- Corporate navigation with clear contact or sales action.
- Trust-led hero: what the company does, for whom, and why it matters.
- Proof strip with measurable credibility.
- Solution or audience cards that route visitors to the next page.

## Required States

- First viewport must communicate company category and offer.
- Include trust proof, not only marketing claims.
- Mobile layout should keep contact/sales action easy to find.

## Avoid

- Abstract hero copy that never says what the company does.
- Stock-like feature grids with no proof.
- Treating corporate pages like SaaS app dashboards.
