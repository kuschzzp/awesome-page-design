# L02 - SaaS Landing Page

## Purpose

Use this framework for product marketing pages that need to explain value, build trust, and guide visitors toward signup, demo, or trial.

## Structure

- Navigation with product identity, links, and primary CTA.
- Hero with clear offer, supporting copy, and action.
- Social proof or customer signals.
- Feature sections grouped by user outcome.
- Product workflow or screenshot area.
- Pricing, plans, or value comparison.
- FAQ and final CTA.

## Required States

- CTA hover/focus states.
- Responsive nav state.
- Pricing toggle state when billing periods exist.
- Form validation when lead capture is present.

## Responsive Behavior

- Keep hero concise on mobile.
- Stack proof, feature, and pricing grids.
- Keep primary CTA visible without sticky clutter.

## Works Well With Visual Styles

D Bento Grid, G Aurora Gradient, E Glassmorphism, P Material You, F Neo-Brutalism, M Resonant Stark.

## Avoid

- Vague headlines.
- Feature grids that repeat the same layout too many times.
- Copying dashboard layouts into a marketing page.
